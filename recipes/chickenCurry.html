Chicken Curry

This chicken curry recipes are easy and very flavorful.

  • 1 kg chicken, cut into serving pieces
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 thumb-size ginger, sliced
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 can (400 ml) coconut milk
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan and sauté onion, garlic, and ginger until fragrant.
  2. Add chicken pieces and cook until they are lightly browned.
  3. Sprinkle curry powder over the chicken and stir well to coat.
  4. Pour in coconut milk and water, then add potatoes. Bring to a boil.
  5. Reduce heat and simmer for 30-40 minutes or until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has thickened.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve hot with rice.
